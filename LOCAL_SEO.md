## 1. Physical Footprint & Local Relevance

Siliguri occupies an irreplaceable role in Himalayan tea commerce. Situated in the plains of the Darjeeling district at the junction of West Bengal, Sikkim, Assam, and Nepal, it serves as the physical gateway to the world's finest tea gardens:

* **Sourcing Proximity:** Located less than 2 hours from the historic estates of Kurseong and Darjeeling, and adjacent to the Dooars and Terai tea belts.
* **Auction & Trade Proximity:** Located within minutes of the Siliguri Tea Auction Centre (STAC), one of India's preeminent orthodox and CTC trading hubs.
* **Logistical Edge:** Immediate access to Bagdogra International Airport (IXB) and New Jalpaiguri Railway Junction (NJP) enables rapid air express dispatch to metros nationwide within 24–48 hours of lot evaluation.
