## 1. Content Mission & E-E-A-T Editorial Standards

In a market saturated with generic blends and marketing hype, Himalayan Tea House establishes deep authority through first-party agricultural truth. Our content demonstrates profound Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness (E-E-A-T):

* **Direct Estate Verification:** All statements regarding bush elevation, clonal cultivar types (AV2, B157, T-78), soil composition, and harvest flush timing are cross-checked against actual factory manufacturing journals from our partner gardens.
* **Sensory Authenticity:** Tasting notes reflect standardized sensory evaluation sessions adhering to Tea Board of India and International Standard ISO 3103 cupping protocols.
* **Zero AI Hallucination:** Editorial guides never make unsubstantiated health panacea claims ("cures illness") or fictitious provenance stories. Every fact is traceable to agricultural science.

## 3. Evidence Sources & Data Provenance

1. **Garden Invoices & Lot Numbers:**
   * Each batch carries an authentic lot number (e.g. `RH-EX-01/26`, `GP-O-04/26`) corresponding to the estate invoice.
2. **Elevation Data:**
   * Measured via garden contour surveys (e.g., Risheehat 4,500–6,200 ft; Giddapahar 4,800 ft; Upper Assam 300 ft).
3. **Cupping Attributes:**
   * Documented across 6 sensory axes (floral, fruity, malty, body, sweetness, earthy) scored 0–5 by our licensed tasting panel.

## 4. Editorial Workflow & Review Cycle

1. **Harvest Ingestion (March, June, October):**
   * Upon arrival of seasonal flushes in Siliguri, cupping notes, liquor photos, and steep specs are recorded.
2. **Article Drafting & Fact-Checking:**
   * Sourcing details verified with garden superintendents.
   * Word count minimum: 800+ substantive words per guide, structured with semantic H2/H3 headings and actionable steeping tables.
3. **Schema & Metadata Verification:**
   * JSON-LD `Article` schema validated prior to publication.
   * `updated_at` timestamps updated only when substantive content or brewing data is refined.
4. **Annual Content Audit:**
   * Annual review in January to retire discontinued lots, update harvest year citations, and ensure internal links resolve with HTTP 200.
