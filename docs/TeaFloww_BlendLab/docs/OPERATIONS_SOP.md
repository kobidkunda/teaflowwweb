# Lab & Fulfillment SOP — Draft for Real-World Validation

**This is a proposed procedure, not evidence of an already operating lab.** Assign a qualified tea taster and operational owner to validate all method details.

## 1. Before a sample arrives
- Confirm payment and service tier.
- Give each project a unique reference (do not put confidential formulas in public labels).
- Confirm pickup coverage/address, inbound weight limit, packaging instructions, and approved shipping request.
- Ask customer to identify any flavoured, allergenic or other ingredients; do not accept unknown hazardous materials.
- Recommend sealed moisture-resistant packaging; published sample mass must support repeat tasting and retention.

## 2. Intake
- Photograph parcel exterior before opening; record carrier, tracking, seal condition, arrival timestamp.
- Record net sample weight in grams; check for water damage, pests, damaged packaging, off-odour and mismatched IDs.
- If unsuitable, quarantine/record exception, contact customer and follow replacement/refund policy.
- Issue QR/internal sample code; store lot and identity chain of custody.
- Maintain sealed retained portion as determined by qualified operations lead.

## 3. Pre-tasting setup
- Use hygienic utensils and safe water.
- Record sample method ID and version for each tea class.
- Record tea dose, water volume, temperature, steep time; for milk-chai use standard milk, water, heating and sweetener conditions.
- Prevent strong aromas/contamination near cupping session.
- Use calibrated scales and temperature devices where a claim depends on those readings.
- Do not issue repeatability/confidence claims without control method or panel design.

## 4. Testing
- Dry-leaf: visual uniformity, grade/particle characteristics, visible fibre, aroma and visible defects.
- Brewed liquor: visual appearance, brightness, aroma, body, briskness, bitterness, finish.
- Milk performance: standardized preparation and subjective strength, colour and balance.
- Record clear free-text observations and defined rubric scores.
- Attach evidence photos with timestamps; note when an observation is not available.

## 5. Analysis and recommendations
- Compare to customer's stated goals and brewing method.
- Identify potential sensory trade-offs in changing proportions/grades.
- Any sourcing quote must use date-stamped lot cost, availability, packaging and loss assumptions.
- For ₹299, provide concise matching/quote notes only; do not claim a detailed PDF entitlement.
- For ₹1,999, publish a detailed report, **not** physical new trial samples.
- For ₹2,999, create planned test formulas only within included time/material scope; log version and lot used.

## 6. Review and report
- Second reviewer checks sample ID, photos, method details, scores, price assumptions, customer goals and factual limitations.
- Reject unsupported claims and missing method evidence.
- Publish immutable PDF version with date, ID, reviewer, checksum.
- Email secure report access to verified customer; record delivery event.

## 7. Trial shipping (Signature only)
- Prepare approved candidate(s) within defined weight and outbound shipment allowance.
- Use traceable, food-safe packaging. Add formula version public label and brewing instructions.
- QC verifies contents, mass, seal and address; then books courier.
- Record dispatch and delivery tracking. Handle damaged/lost shipment according to published policy.

## 8. Feedback/revision
- Customer tastes under recommended brewing method and submits structured feedback.
- Reviewer checks remaining revision entitlement and suitability of requested change.
- Create new formula version; preserve initial version and comparison.
- Do not silently substitute recipe when accepted customer quote is active.

## 9. Quote and bulk supply
- Commercial reviewer checks physical stock, supplier quotes, true MOQ, lead time, losses and packaging.
- Quote expires at clearly stated time; require repricing after expiry.
- Confirm approved formula version before making a bulk order.
- Handoff to existing stock/production/dispatch chain; retain batch traceability.

## 10. Exceptions
- Failed courier pickup → retry or manual agreement, notify customer.
- Sample lost/damaged → defined replacement/refund route; never mark tested.
- Inadequate sample → hold project, request extra material, preserve fee policy.
- Unavailable lot → alternatives must undergo customer approval as applicable.
- Tasting inconsistency → supervisor repeat/review, record version and difference.
- Customer unresponsive → send allowed reminders, archive per policy, no fictional approval.
