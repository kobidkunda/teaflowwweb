# Landing Copy, Email Templates & SEO

## Brand tone
Premium, precise, accessible, commercially useful. Do not over-promise. Avoid fabricated accreditation, exact reproduction and guaranteed savings.

## Hero copy — recommended
**Eyebrow:** TEAFLOWW BLEND LAB  
**Heading:** Your tea. Our expertise. A better way to blend.  
**Subheading:** Send your existing blend for professional tasting, practical quality insights and optional new formula development — designed around your taste and wholesale goals.  
**Primary CTA:** Explore plans from ₹299  
**Secondary CTA:** Preview a sample report

## Homepage short copy
**Heading:** Is your tea as competitive as it could be?  
**Body:** See what an expert taster notices, identify practical improvements and develop a wholesale-ready blend without guessing.  
**CTA:** Explore Blend Lab

## Reassurance microcopy
- `Every sample is identified and tracked through intake.`
- `Sensory evaluation is performed by a human tester.` (only publish if staffing verified)
- `Your report includes method notes and the source of each result.`
- `Quotes depend on actual tea availability, order quantity and final specifications.`

## Transactional email sequence
1. **Payment confirmed:** `Your TeaFloww evaluation reference: {{projectId}}` — paid plan, sample instructions, next action, dashboard link.
2. **Courier booked:** `Your tea sample collection details` — carrier, label instructions, pickup restrictions and customer support.
3. **Sample received:** `Your tea has arrived for evaluation` — receipt condition and sample code.
4. **Evaluation underway:** `Your professional tasting is in progress` — factual stage, no invented completion time.
5. **Report published:** `Your TeaFloww evaluation report is ready` — secure link with expiry and scope caveats.
6. **Trial dispatched:** `Your trial blend is on its way` — tracking, formula public ref, tasting instructions.
7. **Feedback requested:** `What did you think of your trial blend?` — honest choices and revision allowance.
8. **Quote ready:** `Your wholesale tea quotation is available` — price basis, MOQ and validity.
9. **Exception:** `Action required for your sample shipment` — transparent issue and available remedies.

## SEO
Main page `/blend-lab`
- Title: `Professional Tea Testing & Blend Development | TeaFloww`
- Meta description: `Send your tea sample to TeaFloww for professional tasting, actionable quality insights, optional blend development and wholesale sourcing. Services from ₹299.`
- Intent clusters: professional tea tasting service, tea quality evaluation, CTC milk chai blend development, wholesale tea blend matching, private-label tea development.
- Internal links: store header/footer, eligible tea education pages, B2B product collection and related FAQs.
- Structured data: valid Organization, Service, BreadcrumbList; FAQ schema only if suitable and content genuinely visible; do not insert fake reviews or ratings.
- Performance: server-render static content if supported; optimized verified imagery with dimensions; avoid massive animations.

## Real customer case study policy
Before publishing a case study, obtain written customer permission, document evaluation method, comparable base/final cost calculation, ingredient and test date, and outcomes that can be verified. Anonymize supply-chain and customer data appropriately.
