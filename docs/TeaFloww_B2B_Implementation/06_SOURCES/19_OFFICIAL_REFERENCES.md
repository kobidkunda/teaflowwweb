## Last-known observation window and caveats

- Connected product catalog seen 17 Nov 2026: starter retail packs 250g/500g/1kg and bulk 25kg variants existed. **Inventory, price and MOQ can change**, fetch from current API on build.
- Account data from original audit: Meta tea-stall Purchase campaign was ACTIVE with modest spend/clicks, not a B2B qualified-lead performance benchmark. Google CTC campaign returned insufficient lead outcome volume. GA4 had low generic `generate_lead` counts.
- Claims about “3x better Hindi conversions”, “40% spend savings”, “perfect consistency”, “free pickup”, or guaranteed launch profit were *present in source copy* but not verified in the supplied evidence and must not become ad claims without proof.

## Evidence handoff expectation

Before promoting a statement: owner and reviewer, source link or internal proof ID, checked date, markets/format it applies to, expiration or next review, allowed channels (page/ad/sales). If any field missing, use neutral “discuss with team” wording.
