# TeaFloww — Tea Business Partner Acquisition Campaign Pack

**Prepared:** 17 November 2026  
**Status:** Production-ready draft and strategic specification only. **No new ads were launched, no budgets were changed, and no landing pages were published.**  
**Goal:** Qualified tea trading business conversations and paid B2B orders, not consumer tea purchases.  
**Target:** (1) New entrants who want to begin a tea trading/retail brand and (2) existing tea traders, wholesalers, resellers and distributors.

## A. Verified offer and website audit

TeaFloww's public site presents four key sections: `/wholesale`, `/private-label`, `/blend-lab` and `/trials`. The live linked product catalogue lists 250g, 500g and 1kg retail variants; 25kg and 35kg options (as well as 5kg and 10kg). The user confirms TeaFloww offers tea supply, white-label design/branding, and marketing support from Siliguri. The marketing support deliverables are NOT yet specified prominently on the pages reviewed; publish a clear, accurate service scope before advertising it broadly.

**Critical landing-page problems to resolve before paid traffic:**

1. Homepage positions itself as consumer tea discovery rather than a tea-business supply and branding partner.
2. `/private-label` is sophisticated but complex for new founders; a low-friction starter consultation should precede advanced pack configuration.
3. `/wholesale` speaks to specialty cafes, boutique hotels and tea retailers, not directly to aspiring traders and wholesalers.
4. `/trials` presents a four-tea kit with a title saying 4×25g yet its displayed details say 4×50g; the product is consumer-oriented, with ₹200 plus delivery at checkout. Do not use its current version as the business acquisition incentive.
5. Homepage has shown ₹30 starting products while the linked live catalogue listed the 100g SKU at ₹40: reconcile display data before price-led campaigns.
6. Some pages show different free-shipping thresholds (₹999 versus ₹499). Reconcile sitewide announcements.
7. The wholesale public page displays a different WhatsApp number from the private-label page/connected storefront settings. Verify and unify contact routing.
8. Wholesale site says 5kg low trial minimum, Blend Lab describes 26kg wholesale production MOQ, connected settings describe 50kg wholesale MOQ, and print methods have separate unit MOQs. These are distinct offers but need one clear MOQ matrix by product, packing and workflow.
9. Many private-label images are labelled as illustrative AI mockups. Paid ads should use genuine facility and product footage where possible and label conceptual previews accurately.
10. Do not advertise unverified certifications, guaranteed margins, guaranteed launch timelines, free consultation, minimum order quantities, legal support or free samples without commercial verification.

**Relevant connected account snapshot:** Meta currently has an ACTIVE tea stall Purchase campaign: in a 30-day query it spent ₹463.61, generated 111 reported link clicks and 13 reported landing page views; these metrics do not prove 98 failures or poor actual site loading, since pixel instrumentation and page behavior affect the ratio. Google Ads reported an enabled non-brand CTC campaign with 3 impressions, 0 clicks and ₹0 spend on 16 Nov 2026. GA4 for 18 Oct–16 Nov 2026 reported 20 sessions containing `/private-label`, 2 for `/wholesale` and three `generate_lead` events across the site; these are not verified qualified B2B leads. Google Search Console yielded little evidence of organic traffic for direct private-label business-intent queries over 15 Oct–14 Nov.

Market positioning: Comparable Siliguri suppliers publish private-label services, with 50kg and 60kg trial-size propositions; TeaFloww should differentiate through a verifiable, integrated sourcing–branding–packaging–marketing support workflow, not unsupported 'lowest MOQ' or 'highest margin' claims.

## B. The two audience offers

### Beginner / first-time entrepreneur
**Promise:** “Your tea business, step by step: tea selection, bulk or retail packaging, brand design and launch planning support from Siliguri.”  
**Required buyer input:** city/pincode, business stage, format preference, starting quantity band, expected start date, phone.  
**First CTA:** “Get My Tea Business Plan & Quote”.  
**Objection handling:** No existing logo? Can discuss a design brief. No tea knowledge? Can request product guidance. Unsure of quantity? Offer quote by quantity band, and clarify MOQ before ordering.  
**Avoid:** 'Start with ₹X' before full landed costs are verified, or 'guaranteed profit'.

### Existing trader / reseller / distributor
**Promise:** “Reliable bulk supply with optional branded retail packs and custom blend discussions.”  
**Required buyer input:** current monthly tea volume, target grades/taste, required packs, destination, price target, current sourcing pain, expected next order date.  
**First CTA:** “Get Bulk & Private-Label Quote”.  
**Objection handling:** Buyer may supply a sample/specification for Blend Lab if relevant; check batch consistency, dispatch, freight and landed pricing by written quotation.  
**Avoid:** 'Cheapest in India', 'same-day dispatch', 'guaranteed quality match' without evidence.

**Do not** merge tea-stall customer purchase ads with trader startup acquisition; they are different customer journeys. **Do not** launch a brand-new expensive YouTube conversion campaign with only three generic site lead events.

**Budget:** No new campaign budget was authorized. Avoid committing money or splitting a tiny existing budget across too many ad sets. Initially prioritize Google high-intent Search and Meta prospecting videos; add YouTube conversion spend once conversion quality is proven. Budget allocation should follow validated shipping cost, lead-to-order close rate and estimated gross contribution after freight, packing and marketing expenses.

**Locations:** Start with destinations that operations can serve reliably at acceptable landed cost. Use states or city clusters only after calculating actual freight/serviceability. Target 'Presence' rather than broad interest-in-location where that better matches delivery constraints. Use Hindi/Hinglish narration with English subtitles for applicable regions, and localize after observing qualified demand; do not assume every audience speaks Hindi.

## F. Conversion-ready landing page specification

A fast, mobile-first one-page funnel with two prominent selectors: **I want to start** | **I already trade**. Within each path show matching offer, clear packs and MOQ qualifiers, genuine tea and packaging photos, five-step process, a quote flow, transparent freight language, FAQ and trusted business contact.

**Hero copy (beginner):** `Start Your Own Tea Business With TeaFloww` — `Tea supply, retail pouches, branding and practical launch support from Siliguri.` CTA `Build My Tea Business Quote`.

**Hero copy (trader):** `Wholesale Tea Supply. Your Brand. One Partner.` — `Explore bulk sacks, retail pouches, custom blending and private-label formats.` CTA `Get a Trade Quote`.

**Mobile UX rules:** no blocking language modal; optional small language picker; content loads fast on low-cost phones; avoid autoplay video on mobile; compress actual footage, defer media, use large thumb-friendly CTA, short form; low-bandwidth fallback and WhatsApp option. Pricing shown as *indicative only* if freight/tax/packing differs by location and configuration.

**Lead form 5 fields max:** (1) Startup / already trading, (2) Desired bulk / retail / own-label, (3) First-order quantity band 25–49 / 50–99 / 100–249 / 250+ kg, (4) Destination pincode, (5) Name and phone. More granular project details can be gathered on WhatsApp or through an optional step. Capture click IDs and UTMs under consent rules.

**Qualification WhatsApp greeting:** `Hello TeaFloww, I am [starting a business/already trading]. My city/pincode is __, I am interested in [bulk 25kg sacks/250g–1kg retail packs/private label], and my intended first order is __kg. Please share options, minimums and landed quotation.`

**Required trust proofs:** photos of real supply and packed products, verified business identity, real labelled examples with permission, actual sample policies and fees, packaging print method-specific MOQ, written scope for design and marketing services, clear freight/COD/refund policy. No invented testimonials or guaranteed profit examples.

## G. Measurement and optimization framework

Separate consumer retail purchases from B2B lead conversions. Define events as:

| Event | Meaning | Reporting priority |
|---|---|---|
| `business_page_view` | Visitor views business offer | Diagnostic only |
| `business_quote_start` | Starts quote form | Diagnostic only |
| `business_quote_submit` | Genuine completed enquiry, deduplicated | Secondary at first |
| `business_whatsapp_click` | Click-to-chat; not verified chat | Secondary, never equal to a conversation |
| `qualified_business_lead` | Contactable trader with compatible volume and geography | Primary optimization target once volume and import work |
| `business_quote_sent` | Quotation delivered to prospect | Funnel reporting |
| `first_b2b_paid_order` | Verified first commercial order, net of cancellations | North-star outcome |

Attach `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content` and `utm_term`, preserve GCLID/GBRAID/WBRAID or Meta click IDs as applicable, and sync qualified/sold stages from CRM to ad platform only through supported, consent-compliant mechanisms. For Google Ads, use enhanced conversions for leads via Data Manager where applicable. Do not count a `generate_lead` ecommerce form as a qualified trader unless confirmed.

**Success hierarchy:** cost per verified qualified lead (CPQL) → lead-to-quote rate → quote-to-first-order conversion → CAC (spend per first paid B2B customer) → contribution profit after tea cost, packaging, delivery, tax implications and advertising. Use reports by audience, geography, pack format and creative. Do not declare a winner from clicks or 2–3 leads.

**Break-even example, NOT a forecast:** If first-order contribution after COGS, packing, freight, payment losses and variable fulfilment is ₹1,500 and 10% of qualified leads purchase, a theoretical break-even CPQL is ₹150; prudent target needs to be lower to allow overhead and acquisition risk. Replace both variables with actual sales data before setting targets.
