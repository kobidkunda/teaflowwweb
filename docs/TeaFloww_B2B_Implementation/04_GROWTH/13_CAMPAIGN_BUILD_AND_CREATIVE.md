# Meta, Google Search and Google Video — Campaign Build Runbook

> This is a **build specification**, not a report that ads were created. Do not publish or spend until the owner explicitly authorizes specific budgets, regions, accounts, and final creative. Source: `20_ORIGINAL_CAMPAIGN_PACK.md` with claim warnings from `01_DECISIONS_AND_SOURCE_GATES.md`.

## Campaign objective and definition of success

Sell an ongoing **sourcing + packaging + branding support relationship** to two distinct buyers. The first goal is a **contactable, genuinely qualified inquiry**, not a video view, casual WhatsApp link click, or consumer retail Purchase event. The commercial result is a **first paid B2B order** followed by repeat purchase and contribution after freight/packing.

### Separate from existing TeaFloww acquisition

- Preserve tea-stall sales/Purchase campaigns, pixel/catalogue mappings and unrelated budgets.
- Create B2B-specific campaign names, landing pages, forms, CRM attribution and conversion actions.
- Have the sales desk define *eligible kg bands, product type, geography, buying window and purchasing authority* before classifying a lead as qualified.

### Video craft specification

- On-screen caption maximum 5–8 words per frame; keep all critical words away from top and bottom overlay zones.
- First 2 seconds must explicitly identify tea **business**, not consumer drinking tea.
- Film product close-ups at actual size, show human work and inspectable materials; no fictitious lab, factory, endorsements or real-time stock claims.
- Color-grade as warm copper tea liquor plus dark evergreen; use actual tea tones, not neon green.
- Captions remain legible without sound; licensed or original audio only; export 9:16 master and properly reframed 4:5 or 1:1 as needed.
- Motion should be smooth and economical; no heavy transitions or unreadable text.
- Every storyboard output must have explicit sign-offs: product image rights, claim clearance, VO translation, final URL, conversion test.

Use **all exact headline and description assets already documented in `20_ORIGINAL_CAMPAIGN_PACK.md` section E**. ### Responsive Search build checklist

1. Confirm search-only campaign, approved countries/regions, presence settings where available, languages and conversion action.
2. Construct 2 ad groups by purchase intent; enter exact and phrase match with respective negatives.
3. Insert two RSA variants per ad group from the preserved source pack; validate dynamic headlines in interface.
4. Add sitelinks to **real** `/wholesale`, `/private-label`, `/blend-lab`; do not use unpublished pages until deployed.
5. Apply approved tracking suffix; verify live landing with gclid and UTM capture.
6. Keep campaign paused until signed-off offer, creative, privacy, forms and conversion events pass end-to-end tests.

## Attribution naming and sample payload

```ts
// src/lib/business/ad-urls.ts — pure helper used in ad export / QA scripts
export type Channel = 'meta' | 'google_search' | 'google_video';
export type Buyer = 'starter' | 'trader';

export function landingUrl(input: {
  channel: Channel; buyer: Buyer; variant: 'A' | 'B';
  campaign: string; term?: string;
}) {
  const path = input.buyer === 'starter' ? '/start-tea-business' : '/tea-traders';
  const url = new URL(path, 'https://teafloww.com');
  url.searchParams.set('utm_source', input.channel === 'meta' ? 'facebook' : 'google');
  url.searchParams.set('utm_medium', input.channel === 'google_search' ? 'cpc' : 'paid_social');
  url.searchParams.set('utm_campaign', input.campaign);
  url.searchParams.set('utm_content', `${input.buyer}_${input.variant}`);
  if (input.term) url.searchParams.set('utm_term', input.term);
  return url.toString();
}
```

**Important:** Google auto-tagging and click IDs require separate retention/consent handling. The helper is a preview builder, **not** a substitute for platform tracking templates. If Meta placement differs (Facebook vs Instagram), prefer platform-supported dynamic source parameters with QA. No unverified fees or business claims belong in dynamic templates.

## Launch readiness and experiment principle

- Pre-register a hypothesis: A vs B changes *message angle*, not both targeting and landing page simultaneously.
- Assess CPQL and qualified-to-quote-to-paid order progression after enough real outcomes; compare quality-adjusted cost by segment, format and location.
- Run only as many parallel tests as budget supports. First outcome should be learned qualification, not guaranteed low CPA.
- Freeze broad geographic expansion until logistics verifies freight and delivery viability for each destination band.
