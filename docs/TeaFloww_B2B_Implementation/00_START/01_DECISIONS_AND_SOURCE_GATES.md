# Source Reconciliation, Assumption Register & Commercial Fact Gates

## Basis

**Source A:** `20_ORIGINAL_CAMPAIGN_PACK.md` — evidence-cautious campaign specification with two buyer segments, four master videos, four RSAs and strong data-quality caveats.  
**Source B:** `21_SECOND_UPLOADED_AUDIT.md` — more assertive 2-version creative framework, MOQ/packing/dispatch/Blend Lab claims and specific prices.  
**This blueprint:** reconciles the two without silently converting marketing claims into operational facts.

## Authority precedence

1. Signed-off operational/current live database and verified public price/offer page.
2. Confirmed owner statement for operational capabilities (sourcing, packing, branding, design, marketing support).
3. Existing cautious campaign pack where claims conflict.
4. Second uploaded audit for **creative inspiration only** where it makes unverified numerical or absolute claims.
5. Future hypotheses must be labeled **proposed** and measured, not promoted as proven.

## Separate customer outcomes

**Beginner lead:** plan + setup proposal + sample/brand discovery. No promise that business will succeed.  
**Trader lead:** grade/sample/pack specification + freight-inclusive commercial quotation. No promised price/quality match until laboratory and sample approval.

- 'Share your destination and intended volume for a tailored quotation.'
- 'Custom packaging and blend development are subject to minimums, sample approval and availability.'

## Do not copy blindly from the uploaded ads

The second audit includes a compelling **three-option narrative** but describes alleged 3x conversion advantages and guaranteed results. Keep the storytelling and packs, remove the unverified metrics, price claims and hard guarantees. Source A's four-master-ad design (two variations per segment) is the robust launch model. The original two umbrella scripts from source B may be reused only as initial ideation, with claim review.
