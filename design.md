# Design System — Contemporary Himalayan Tea House

## North star
An editorial, sensory, evidence-led premium tea storefront. Quiet luxury, authentic provenance and an effortless path to purchase. The design should never rely on fake scarcity, unverified claims, exaggerated wellness promises or permanent discount banners.

## Visual identity
- Paper: #F6F1E7
- Raised paper: #FBF8F1
- Ink: #171713
- Forest: #1D2A24
- Pine: #23483A
- Moss: #697A55
- Amber: #B36B3D
- Brass: #B39156
- Mist: #DDE4DF
- Stone: #C9C2B6

Use 60–70% warm paper, restrained dark green, and brass only as a small accent. The starter uses system sans and Georgia as a license-free placeholder. Replace with properly licensed editorial serif and commerce sans after brand sign-off.

## Layout
- Editorial maximum width: 1440–1600px
- Commerce width: 1320–1440px
- Desktop: 12-column underlying grid, 24–32px gutters
- Mobile: 16–20px gutters
- Desktop section spacing: 72–112px
- Mobile section spacing: 48–72px
- Prefer restrained corners (0–12px), not excessive pills.

## Core hierarchy
Desire → Understanding → Certainty → Action.
Hero: one statement, one proof sentence, one primary CTA.
PLP: image, name, origin, tasting notes, price, weight/cups, quick add.
PDP: identity, sensory notes, variants, price-per-cup, delivery, add-to-bag, Tea Passport, Taste Map, brewing and reviews.
Checkout: minimal and explicit, with guest purchase and no forced account.

## Signature components
- Tea Passport: origin, estate, harvest, lot, cultivar, processing, packed date and verified certifications.
- Taste Map: intensity bars with textual equivalents.
- Tea Finder: novice-friendly guided choices, not fake match percentages.
- Gift Atelier: recipient, occasion, budget and custom presentation.
- Tea Society: future subscriptions and early access.

## Imagery
Hero caddy, dry leaf macro, standardized liquor, lifestyle cup, estate, maker, brewing and gift unboxing. All provenance images must be attributable. Current illustrations are placeholders and not photographs.

## Accessibility
Target WCAG 2.2 AA. 4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large text, meaningful focus, semantic controls, keyboard operability, reduced motion, 44px practical touch targets, descriptive alt text, accessible status announcements. Test in actual browsers.

## Performance
Field targets at p75: LCP <=2.5s, INP <=200ms, CLS <=0.1. Avoid blocking videos, huge hero assets, layout shifts and excessive client JS.

## Motion
Microfeedback 120–180ms; drawers 180–260ms; editorial reveals 300–500ms. Honor prefers-reduced-motion.

## Content ethics
Every statement about an estate, certification, GI tag, producer, lot, review, quantity or delivery must come from verified operational data. This starter intentionally marks sample provenance as illustrative.
