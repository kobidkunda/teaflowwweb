# Teafloww Logo #9 — TRUE VECTOR MASTER KIT

40 production-oriented logo variations supplied as independent SVG files in `SVG/`.

## Technical verification
- Logo emblem: deliberately rebuilt as hand-authored, smooth Bezier `<path>` elements.
- Lettering and taglines: glyph geometry is converted to filled vector `<path>` elements, not fonts or HTML text.
- No embedded or linked bitmap images, base64 imagery, screenshot tracing objects, HTML, or external image dependencies.
- Scalable without resolution/pixelation. Editable in Inkscape, Illustrator, CorelDRAW and other SVG editors.
- Source SVGs have either transparent background or intentional vector fill backdrop (as named).
- Color palette and individual fills can be changed directly; all files are layered groups/shapes.

## Faithfulness and limits
These designs faithfully follow the **style, geometry and wording** of the previously shared Teafloww #9 branding concept (the leaf-and-hills version), but are a **manual vector recreation**, not a mathematically pixel-perfect reconstruction. Original was only raster AI artwork; no original curves or master font specifications existed. Before mass-production, compare enlarged reference visually and approve curves, wordmark proportions and print trapping on actual substrate.

## Important note on #9
The original 100-logo grid’s #9 was a sunrise/tea-terraces emblem WITHOUT the right-hand leaf. The subsequent approved branding identity board reinterpreted it by adding a leaf. THIS kit corresponds to that latest branding identity board. If you want the original #9 without the leaf, ask for a separate faithful vector edition.

## Printing notes
- For **gravure/flexo single-color printing**, begin with `14_monochrome_black`, `31_single_color_forest`, or `35_horizontal_black` and map the entire logo to your actual spot ink.
- For **foil stamping, embossing or debossing**, use flat solid-color artwork. A stamp/emboss die-maker must validate fine gaps and minimum strokes; preview shadows are not die specs.
- The background color of an SVG without a rectangle is transparent. White logo on transparent canvas may be hard to see on white screens.
- Gradients intentionally omitted in the foil versions, as print tooling benefits from solid paths.
- Branding concept is not trademark clearance or a guarantee of legal availability.
