# TeaFloww product context

TeaFloww is Nirva’s tea commerce brand at TeaFloww.com. Its purpose is a premium, accessible, fast tea buying experience with truthful sourcing and simple buying journeys. Business enquiries use info@teafloww.com and +91 7001304853; company details remain governed by the repository’s approved brand information.

Customers include tea buyers, beginners exploring a tea business, and existing traders seeking supply. Help beginners understand decisions and costs while giving experienced buyers a direct quotation route. Present catalogue-backed products, sample prices and availability; confirm trade terms through the existing enquiry process.

Trust is a product requirement: never invent supplier rates, scarcity, certifications, provenance, reviews, case studies or delivery promises. Planning tools explain user-entered assumptions; they do not establish a quotation, legal compliance, profitability or sufficient capital. Server catalogue and order validation remain authoritative.

The established visual system is forest, ivory, restrained brass, Georgia and Arial, documented in `design.md`. Extend that identity across surfaces. The `/start-tea-business` launch studio is documented in `docs/ui/start-tea-business/launch-studio.md`; its page composition is local to that surface.

The `/tea-traders` surface helps new resellers, retailers, distributors and own-brand buyers select a model, compare actual catalogue formats, plan costs and prepare an informed enquiry. Public catalogue pricing supports planning; negotiated rates, freight, payment and delivery terms require the authoritative business and checkout workflows. Its surface context and delivery boundaries are recorded in `docs/ui/tea-traders-redesign/trader-studio.md`.
